# Editorial scoring rubric (proposed; not platform metrics)

Each of the six dimensions receives an integer rating from 0 (no case) to 5 (strong case). The weighted total is 0–100. Scores represent an editor's provisional judgment and need dated sources; they are not the probability of success.

| Field | Weight | What a 5 means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| audience_need | 25 | Several clear user questions or needs, with traceable context |
| expertise_fit | 20 | Host has specific experience, research or a credible interview path |
| discussion_depth | 20 | Substantive question with competing perspectives and enough material for 20–30 minutes |
| originality | 15 | Distinct thesis, overlooked tension or cross-market angle with evidence |
| distribution | 10 | Clear, honest hooks for audio, video and social distribution |
| feasibility | 10 | Sources, access, production and fact-checking are realistically available |

Formula: sum(weight × rating / 5). No observed view counts or search volumes are calculated by the script.

## Decision policy

- editorial_review: two or more distinct source URLs AND two or more distinct named platforms. Still requires a human source check and approval.
- needs_evidence: source diversity gate is unmet. A provisional score can be shown for planning but does not make the candidate production-ready.
- china_global: only when candidate evidence includes both CN and GLOBAL markets.
- An item with missing or unverifiable sources cannot be represented as an established current trend.
- Score is not performance prediction. Use a published show's actual comparable engagement data to revise strategy, never fabricated benchmarks.
